Features

  • Play anywhere

    Vectorwake runs on desktop and mobile. Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls all fly the same browser client.

  • Live multiplayer

    Fight in live rooms with players and bots spread across small teams.

  • Battle bots

    Bots keep the game fun and vibrant when no humans are around. They fill empty seats and leave as human players arrive.

  • Ratings and ranks

    Vectorwake uses an ELO-based rating system to rank you against other pilots. Increase your rank to build reputation.

  • Free and source available

    Vectorwake is free and will remain so. The source is available on GitHub under a noncommercial license.

  • Inertial flight

    Vectorwake uses a simple but fun physics engine based on inertial flight. Carry speed through turns, line up for narrow corridors, and bounce off walls.

  • Ship upgrades

    Collect greens to level guns and bombs, add multifire, bounce, proximity, or shrapnel, and pick up repels, bursts, and mines.

  • Fly as a gunner

    Attach to a teammate and aim from their ship, or carry your friends as gunners while you fly.
