Features

  • Play anywhere

    Open the arena on desktop or mobile. Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls all fly the same browser client.

  • Live multiplayer

    Fight in live rooms with players and AI spread across small teams. New rooms open as the fleet fills.

  • Seven ships

    Fly an interceptor, bomber, skirmisher, heavy, stealth ship, brawler, or area-denial hull. Each has its own shape, weapons, and job.

  • Inertial flight

    Momentum never bleeds away. Carry speed through turns, line up for narrow corridors, and bounce off walls.

  • One energy pool

    Energy is both health and ammunition. A hard volley leaves less of your ship between you and the next hit.

  • Midfight upgrades

    Collect greens to level guns and bombs, add multifire, bounce, proximity, or shrapnel, and pick up repels, bursts, and mines.

  • Fly as a gunner

    Attach to a teammate and aim from their hull, or carry independently aimed gunners while you fly.

  • Fair AI

    Bots see the same arena and send the same controls as everyone else. They fill empty seats and leave them to arriving players.

  • Persistent pilots

    Keep your call sign and rating between games. Claim your pilot with a password to bring the same career to another device.

  • Free and open source

    Vectorwake is free and will remain so. The source is available on GitHub.
