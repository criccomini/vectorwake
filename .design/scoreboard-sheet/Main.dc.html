The scoreboard sheet
The menu's panel, holding the room: a section per side that is also the press that joins it, the pilots under each with what they did and how long they have been in, and the watchers at the foot. It is a stop in the column, its answer is where you stand, and at the whistle it rises on its own with the result as its head.

A rule over a section, as a band has, so it heads a group. The name spoken at 17 in the side's color, the score read beside it, stands or flags after that, seats at the right end. The whole row lights under a hand at the cursor wash, edge to edge, like every row. Pilots indent under it. Pressing another side's row is joining it, gated as a hull change is: full bar, and a respawn.
