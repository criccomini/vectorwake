What the add field says back

The one press whose consequence is invisible. Four answers, on the line under the field.

you typedwhat happenedthe line says
a name nobody holdsnothing No pilot called that.
somebody who has not added youan edge, one way Added. You are friends when they add you back.
somebody already waiting on youthe pair closes Friends. They had already added you.
your own call signnothing That is you.

Two decisions this reverses

docs/design/friends.md rules both of these out by name. Worth seeing on one screen before it is built, because the arguments it makes are still true; they are just outweighed by wanting the feature.

"You cannot type a call sign"

The doc has no text field anywhere, so a friend is a selection off a roster you are already reading. That kept one property: the only people the system will ever show you are people you played with and people who chose to add you.

A field does not break that outright, because you have to know the exact call sign already, and names are a word and three digits. What it does open is an unwanted add landing in a stranger's list.

"There is no accept and no decline"

Adding somebody who has already added you is accepting, so the doc removed the accept screen and with it the inbox a stranger could fill.

An explicit accept brings that inbox back. Ignore is what pays for it: a stranger who has been ignored is off the list that asks for a decision, and stays off it.

What it costs the database

One column. friends is a pair of one-way rows today and the friendship is the pair. An ignore is a third state on the incoming row, which is a flag on the receiving side rather than a new table.

Everything already in /v1/friends stays; the reply grows an ignored list and the add route grows a by name lookup.

Naming

"All" was the word in the brief and it is drawn here as everybody who added you, because "all" on its own does not say all of what, sitting under two other lists.

If it should hold everyone you have ever had an edge with in either direction, friends included, that is a different list and a longer one. Say which and it changes.

Still open

Three things I would want decided before writing it. Does ignoring tell them? Nothing in this game tells anybody anything, so no, and the ignored pilot goes on seeing "added" on their side forever. Can an ignored pilot add you again? They already have; there is no second add to make, so ignore has to be a state and not a deletion, which is what the "everybody" list is for. Does the roster of your room still offer add? Yes, and it should: that is the moment a friend is actually made, and the field is for the one you flew with last week.
